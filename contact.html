For Schools & Libraries

If you represent a school, library, or community organization and would like to bring the Bottor Inventor Workshop to your students — as an in-school residency, a library workshop, or an after-school program — we would love to hear from you. Please use the contact form or email us directly.

For Donors

Have a question about donating or want to learn more about how your support funds prototype materials, Inventor's Journals, and sponsored workshop seats? Visit our Donate page or send us a message.

Volunteer & Get Involved

Interested in volunteering your time or skills to support the Bottor Inventor Workshop? We welcome community members who want to help at a workshop cohort or Inventors Expo. Reach out and let us know how you'd like to be involved.
