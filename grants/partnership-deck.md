# Bottor Learning Foundation — Partnership Deck
### Bring the Bottor Inventor Workshop to Your School, Library, or Community Center

> *"The Bottor Inventor Workshop is a five-day invention-based learning experience where students identify real-world problems, build prototypes, strengthen literacy, and present innovative solutions."*

This is the reusable base deck for approaching schools, libraries, and community centers about hosting the Bottor Inventor Workshop. Copy and adapt per organization — trim to fit a one-page leave-behind or a short slide deck as needed. See the checklist at the bottom before using this for the first time.

---

## What the Workshop Is

The Bottor Inventor Workshop is Bottor Learning Foundation's flagship five-day program. Students in grades 3–5 (ages 8–11) identify a real-world problem, design and build a prototype, test and refine it, and pitch it publicly at a culminating Inventors Expo — with literacy woven into every day through The BOTTOR Framework and the Innovation Vocabulary Challenge.

## What Your Organization Provides

- A space for the week — a classroom, library meeting room, or community space
- Promotion to families and students — a flyer home, a sign-up sheet, or an announcement
- A point of contact to coordinate scheduling
- If choosing the partner-led model: staff willing to be trained on The BOTTOR Framework curriculum

## What Bottor Learning Foundation Provides

- The full five-day curriculum and lesson plans
- Prototype-building materials for every student
- A printed Bottor Inventor's Journal for every student
- Either Bottor staff facilitators leading the sessions directly, or training and ongoing curriculum support for your own educators
- Certificates, recognition awards, and Inventors Expo materials for Day 5
- All of this at **no cost to your school or library** — Bottor Learning Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and partner-hosted sessions are free to the host thanks to donor support

## What Students Walk Away With

Every student leaves with a completed Inventor's Journal, prototype, invention sketch, project pitch, and a Certificate of Completion — along with greater confidence in reading, problem-solving, creativity, and public speaking.

## Two Ways to Partner

1. **In-School Residency** — delivered during the school day across five days, or spread across after-school sessions to fit your calendar.
2. **Library or Community Center Workshop** — a standalone community program, open for registration or by sign-up.

## Before Using This Deck — Checklist

1. **Confirm facilitator training capacity** before promising a start date for the partner-led model — check Bottor's current training materials and timeline first.
2. **Confirm cohort scheduling availability** before naming a specific semester or date to a prospective partner.
3. **HQ mailing address** — resolved: 11411 W. Park Place, Suite 202, Milwaukee, WI 53224 (see Next Steps above). Use this in any formal partnership agreement or MOU; never substitute the school/library's own address for Bottor's.
4. **No signed partner MOU/agreement template exists yet** — draft one covering space, liability, and scheduling expectations before finalizing the first formal partnership.
5. **Real photos** — swap in an actual Inventors Expo or workshop photo as soon as one exists; this deck currently has none to include.
